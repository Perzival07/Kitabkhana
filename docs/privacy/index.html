Kitabkhana privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Kitabkhana is a private library for the books you own, lend and love. The short version: your library stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking.

What Kitabkhana keeps

Everything you put into the app: your books, where they live, who you lent them to (names, phone numbers and notes you type), your reading progress, quotes, goals, tags, cover pictures and settings. It is stored only on your phone or in your browser and is never sent to us. We do not have a server that holds your library, so we cannot see it, read it or recover it.

What leaves your device

Only two kinds of thing, and only for the reasons below.

1. Book lookups on Open Library. When you scan a barcode, type an ISBN or search by title, Kitabkhana asks Open Library (run by the Internet Archive) for the book's details, and downloads its cover from covers.openlibrary.org, which may pass you on to archive.org. Those requests contain only the ISBN or the title you searched for, plus what any web request contains: your network address and the app's name and version (Kitabkhana/1.0.0). They never contain your library, your name, a borrower's name or a phone number. Open Library's own privacy policy applies to what it does with those requests. If you never use lookup, nothing is sent.

2. Things you choose to share. When you share a backup file, a spreadsheet export, a quote picture, a diagnostics file, or send a reminder to a borrower through WhatsApp or SMS, the file or message goes where you send it. Backups and exports contain your whole library, including borrowers' names and phone numbers, so keep them somewhere private.

Your camera and photos

  • Scanning barcodes uses the camera live; nothing is recorded or kept.
  • Reading a quote from a page uses on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit running on your phone). The photo is read on the device and then thrown away; only the text you choose to save is kept.
  • Covers. A photo you take or choose as a cover is shrunk and kept inside the app. Nothing is uploaded.

The app asks for camera permission only when you open a feature that needs it, and works without it (you can type instead).

Notifications

Loan and reading reminders are scheduled on your device and shown by your device. They need no server. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings or in Kitabkhana's Settings.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Kitabkhana contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking code, and no third-party library that sends information about you anywhere. There is an optional "Send crash reports" switch in Settings. It is off by default, and in this version it is not connected to any service, so nothing is sent whether it is on or off. If crash reporting is ever added, this page will say who receives what, and the switch will stay off until you turn it on.

Backups and the cloud

Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device copy are turned off for Kitabkhana, so your library is not copied to Google's servers behind your back. To move or keep a copy of your library, use Settings: Share a backup (a .kitab file) or Export as spreadsheet. Automatic weekly backups stay in the app's own private storage on your phone, and only the newest three are kept.

The web version

On the web, your library lives in your browser's own storage on your computer or phone. Clearing the site's data removes it. The same two rules apply: only Open Library lookups go out, and only the ISBN or title you searched for. The site loads no third-party scripts, fonts or analytics.

Your control

  • See and keep your data: Settings, Share a backup, or Export as spreadsheet.
  • Delete your data: Settings, Erase all data. It removes every book, loan, quote, setting, cover and backup kept on the device, and pending reminders. Uninstalling the app removes it too. We hold no copy, so there is nothing else to delete.
  • Choose what to share: nothing is shared unless you do it.

Children

Kitabkhana is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. It collects nothing about anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts to do something this page does not describe, this page will change first and the date at the top will move. The app's change log lists those changes too.
