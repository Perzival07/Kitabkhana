# Accessibility

What Kitabkhana does for people who use screen readers, keyboards, large text
or reduced motion, how it is tested, and the manual checks that still need a
person with a device.

## What is built in

* **Screen readers.** Book tiles read as one sentence ("Middlemarch, by George
  Eliot, Reading, Lent out, at Living room shelf"). Progress rings and bars
  speak their value ("5 of 12 books, 42 percent"). Charts have a text summary
  ("Books finished per month: 9 books"). Decorative artwork is hidden from
  screen readers. Section titles are marked as headings.
* **Not colour alone.** Reading status has an icon (filled when chosen) as well
  as colour and words, on the detail page and on library tiles; the starter
  places in onboarding show a tick; due dates are worded ("Overdue by 3 days"),
  not just red.
* **Touch targets** are at least 48dp (checked against both Android's and iOS's
  guidelines on every main screen).
* **Contrast** meets WCAG AA in both themes: `test/app/theme/contrast_test.dart`
  checks every text and icon pairing the app draws, and Flutter's
  `textContrastGuideline` runs on each screen.
* **Large text.** Every main screen is exercised at 320dp wide and 200% text
  with no overflow; wide rows switch to vertical or scroll (the Loans tabs
  scroll, reading cards stack).
* **Keyboard.** Every control is reachable by Tab; focused buttons show a 3dp
  ring in the text colour; Escape closes bottom sheets and dialogs.
* **Reduced motion.** Every animation goes through `MotionPrefs`
  (`lib/ui/motion.dart`), which follows the system setting: movement stops and
  durations become zero.

## Automated checks

`test/a11y/accessibility_test.dart` runs Flutter's `androidTapTargetGuideline`,
`iOSTapTargetGuideline`, `labeledTapTargetGuideline` and
`textContrastGuideline` on: Library (grid and list), Add, Book Detail, Scan,
Reading, Stats, More, Quotes, Loans, Locations, Settings, Tags, About,
Onboarding and the quote capture screen, plus the Update-page and Lend sheets,
each in light and dark. It also checks 200% text on every screen and Escape
closing a sheet.

## Screen-reader walkthrough (TalkBack and VoiceOver)

Do this on a real device with the reader on, in the **dev** flavour. Each step
says what you should hear and do. Mark any step where something is silent,
wrong, or in a confusing order.

Setup: Android: Settings > Accessibility > TalkBack. iOS: Settings >
Accessibility > VoiceOver. Swipe right/left to move, double-tap to activate.

### 1. Add a book
1. Library tab: you hear "Library, tab 1 of 5". Move to the add button:
   "Add a book, button". Activate.
2. Choose "Type ISBN": the field announces its label. Enter `9780441013593`
   and activate "Look up".
3. The preview announces the title and author. Move through the fields (title,
   authors, pages, location). Choose a location: the picker lists places and
   says which is selected.
4. Activate "Add to library". You hear the toast ("Added to Living room
   shelf") and focus lands on the library.
5. Find the book in the list: it reads as one item with title, authors, status
   and place.

### 2. Scan
1. Scan tab. Grant camera access. The screen explains what it is for.
2. Point at a barcode of a book you own. You hear a short tone/vibration and
   "You own this" followed by where it is (live region).
3. Move to "Open book" and "Scan another"; both are labelled buttons.
4. Dismiss with the system back gesture; scanning resumes.
5. Alternatively use "Search by title": type a title; results are announced.

### 3. Lend
1. Open a book you own. Move to "Lend", activate.
2. The sheet announces "Lend or borrow". Enter a name; the due-date choices
   announce which is selected ("2 weeks, selected").
3. Activate "Save". You hear "Lent to Priya".
4. The detail page now says it is lent; status and location read correctly.

### 4. Return
1. Loans tab (More > Loans). Tabs announce their counts ("Lent out, 1").
2. Open the loan's menu ("More") and choose "Return". Confirm.
3. You hear that the book is back and where it was put.
