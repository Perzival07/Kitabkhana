Kitabkhana is a private home for the books you own, lend and love. Add a book in seconds, know exactly where it lives, and never buy it twice. There is no account and no sign-up: your library stays on your phone.

NEVER BUY A BOOK TWICE
Standing in a shop? Scan the barcode and Kitabkhana tells you at once whether you already own it, whether it's on your wishlist, whether you lent it to a friend, or whether you have a different edition. It works offline for everything you already have, and finds a book by title when there is no barcode.

KNOW WHERE EVERY BOOK LIVES
Give your books places: the living-room shelf, the study desk, the box under the bed. Move a book in a tap and see everything on a shelf at a glance.

LEND WITHOUT LOSING
Note who has your book and when it is due. Kitabkhana reminds you on the day, and can send your friend a gentle WhatsApp or SMS nudge. When the book comes back, it returns to its shelf by itself.

READ MORE, WITH A GENTLE PUSH
Track the page you are on, see your reading streak and how many days a book will take at your pace, and set a yearly goal. Pretty charts show your year in books, with no pressure.

KEEP THE LINES YOU LOVE
Photograph a page and tap the lines you want: the text is read on your phone, tidied up, and saved with its page number. Share a quote as a beautiful card.

YOUR BOOKS, YOUR DEVICE
Everything is stored on your device. No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Only the ISBN or title you search for goes to Open Library to fetch book details. Back up to a file you control, export a spreadsheet, or erase everything in one go.

MADE FOR EVERY SCREEN
Light and dark themes, large text, screen-reader support, phones and tablets, and a web version that works offline.

Free of ads and subscriptions. Questions or ideas? Use Settings, then Send feedback.
