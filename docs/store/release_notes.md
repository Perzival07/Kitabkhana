# Release notes

## 1.0.0

Kitabkhana is here: a private library for the books you own, lend and love.

* Add books by barcode, ISBN, title search or by hand
* Scan in a shop to see if you already own a book
* Shelves and places, tags and search (English, Hindi and Bengali)
* Lend and borrow with reminders and WhatsApp or SMS nudges
* Reading progress, streaks, a yearly goal and charts
* Capture quotes from a photo, share them as cards
* Backup and restore, spreadsheet export, erase everything
* Light and dark themes, large text and screen reader support
* Your library stays on your device: no account, no ads, no tracking
